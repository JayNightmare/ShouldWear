Privacy

ShouldWear has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Stored on your device

Your location, personalisation answers, and the last forecast are kept in your browser's local storage so the app works offline. Clear site data in your browser to remove them.

Sent to third parties

  • Open-Meteo receives your coordinates to return the forecast.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives your coordinates, or the place name you search for, to look up location names.

Your device location is only requested when you choose "Use current location", or when you previously allowed it.
